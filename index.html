07 — FAQ

Questions about websites?

Here are answers to some common questions about website design, development, pricing, responsive design and working with Kritarth Barnwal.

What kind of websites do you build?
I build modern, responsive websites for creators, students, personal brands and small businesses. This includes landing pages, business websites, portfolio websites and website redesigns.
How much does a website cost?
Website pricing depends on the number of pages, design requirements, features and overall project scope. I provide a clear quote after understanding what you need. You can contact me with your project details to discuss it.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
Yes. Responsive design is an important part of the websites I build. The layout is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.
How long does a website take to build?
A typical small website can take around 5–10 days depending on the number of pages, content, design requirements and features. Larger or more detailed projects may take longer.
Can you redesign an existing website?
Yes. I can redesign an existing website with a more modern visual style, responsive layouts, clearer structure and improved user experience.
Do you build websites for small businesses?
Yes. I create professional websites for small businesses that want a stronger online presence and a clear way for customers to learn about their services and get in touch.
Can you help deploy my website?
Yes. I can help prepare and deploy a website so it is ready to be accessed online. I can also help with the basic setup needed for a website hosted through services such as GitHub Pages.
How can I start a website project with you?
Start by sending me a message through the contact form with a few details about your project, what kind of website you need and what you want it to achieve. I'll get back to you from there.
