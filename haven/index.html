10 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

How do I book an appointment? +

Use the appointment form above or contact the clinic directly by phone or WhatsApp.

What should I bring to my first visit? +

Bring any relevant previous dental records, prescriptions or medical information that may help your dentist understand your history.

Do you treat children? +

Pediatric care can be offered depending on the clinic's services and the dentist's qualifications.

How long does a consultation take? +

Appointment length varies depending on the reason for your visit and the assessment required.

Do you offer emergency appointments? +

Emergency availability depends on the clinic schedule. Contact the clinic directly for the latest availability.

Can I request a specific dentist? +

Availability for a specific dentist depends on the clinic's schedule. You can mention your preference when requesting an appointment.
